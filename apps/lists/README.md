# Brightdesk Lists

<p align="center"><img src="../../assets/icons/lists.svg" alt="Brightdesk Lists application icon" width="96" height="96"></p>

Organise inventories, projects and other structured records.

This is the Lists feature area within the Brightdesk Office Suite. The approved product lineup and shared design rules are documented in the [main README](../../README.md) and [visual standard](../../docs/BRIGHTDESK_VISUAL_STANDARD.md).


## Customer Help

The shared Brightdesk Suite shell provides a small, discreet **Help** link near the bottom of the persistent sidebar. For Lists, it opens the central read-only guide at `https://help.brightdesk.com/products/lists.html` in the customer's normal browser. This is the reserved customer address, not a live or verified website yet. Approved Wiki guide updates appear the next time Help is opened and do not require a new Brightdesk installer. Opening Help sends no document or product data.
