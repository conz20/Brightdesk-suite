Getting Started / Architecture

Architecture Overview

The Brightdesk Office Suite is an npm workspaces monorepo containing the integrated Suite and exactly ten approved product workspaces, together with shared design and engineering packages.

Core Principles

  • Ten approved products: Pages, Sheets, Decks, Notes, Database, Forms, Lists, Planner, To Do and Workspace are the only customer products. Each has its own product workspace, name, original icon and interaction patterns.
  • Integrated Suite windows: Opening a product from Home creates or focuses its own separate top-level window inside the Suite process and shared local data store. It does not replace Home, Apps or Files. Separately packaged product installations retain their own native application identity.
  • Original Brightdesk design: Use Brightdesk's own icons, typography, colours and interaction system. Microsoft productivity conventions may inform familiar workflows, but no other product's logos, code or distinctive trade dress are copied.
  • Local-first by default: Documents and product data remain on this device. Online access is limited to approved Brightdesk update and language-resource services.
