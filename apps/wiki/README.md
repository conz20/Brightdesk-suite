This legacy module is not part of the approved Brightdesk Office Suite. The current product scope contains Pages, Sheets, Decks, Notes, Database, Forms, Lists, Planner, To Do and Workspace only.

Do not present this module as a Brightdesk application or add it to the launcher. See the [approved product scope](../../PRODUCT_SCOPE_LOCK.md).


## Customer Help distinction

This legacy module is not the customer Help centre and must remain outside the approved ten-product lineup. Customer Help is maintained separately in Brightdesk Wiki as a free, read-only website. The in-product Help links open the published Wiki guides in the customer's browser; they do not load or use this legacy module. The reserved customer address is `https://help.brightdesk.com`, which is not live or verified yet.
