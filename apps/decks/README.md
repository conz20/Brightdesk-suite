## Customer Help

The shared Brightdesk Suite shell provides a small, discreet **Help** link near the bottom of the persistent sidebar. For Decks, it opens the central read-only guide at `https://help.brightdesk.com/products/decks.html` in the customer's normal browser. This is the reserved customer address, not a live or verified website yet. Approved Wiki guide updates appear the next time Help is opened and do not require a new Brightdesk installer. Opening Help sends no document or product data.
