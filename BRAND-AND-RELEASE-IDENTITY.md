# Brightdesk Brand and Official Release Identity

**Status:** Project identity and release-presentation policy  
**Applies to:** Brightdesk name, product names, logos, icons, visual identity and official release presentation

## Official project identity

The project name is **Brightdesk** (one word, capital B). The ten approved customer products are Pages, Sheets, Decks, Notes, Database, Forms, Lists, Planner, To Do and Workspace.

Brightdesk-AI is private internal engineering tooling, not a customer product. Brightdesk-Wiki is a separate help website.

## Official and modified distributions

- Official Brightdesk downloads are releases published through Brightdesk's designated release channels.
- Modified or independently repackaged distributions must not imply Brightdesk endorsement, sponsorship or official status.
- Do not alter an official package and then present it as an unmodified Brightdesk release.
- Do not use Brightdesk branding in a misleading way or to impersonate the project.
- Factual references needed to identify the source of MIT-licensed code remain subject to the applicable licence and law.

## Original brand assets

Use original Brightdesk artwork in accordance with the project's published asset and release rules. Third-party fonts, icons, images and other assets must retain their required attribution and licence notices. A Brightdesk label does not convert third-party material into Brightdesk-owned material.

## Authenticity

Checksums, signed packages and provenance records can help users identify official releases. They do not make local copies impossible to modify. Release signing and customer-side verification must not be described as active until signed releases and verification steps have actually been implemented and tested.
