# Brightdesk Brand and Official Release Identity

**Status:** Project identity policy  
**Applies to:** Brightdesk name, product names, logos, icons, visual identity and official release presentation

## Project name

The project name is **Brightdesk** (one word, capital B). The ten customer products are Pages, Sheets, Decks, Notes, Database, Forms, Lists, Planner, To Do and Workspace.

Brightdesk-AI is private internal engineering tooling, not a customer product. Brightdesk-Wiki is a separate help website.

## Source-code licences and brand identity are separate

A repository's MIT licence applies to the software covered by that licence and grants the permissions stated in its complete notice. This policy does not remove, narrow or replace those permissions. Third-party components, fonts, dictionaries, artwork and other resources remain governed by their own licences and notices.

The MIT licence does not, by itself, grant a general right to use Brightdesk names, logos or visual identity to suggest that a modified, repackaged or independently distributed build is an official Brightdesk release.

## Official and modified distributions

- Official Brightdesk downloads are those published through Brightdesk's designated release channels.
- Modified or independently repackaged distributions should use a distinct product name and visual identity and must not imply Brightdesk endorsement or official status.
- Do not alter an official package and then present it as an unmodified Brightdesk release.
- Do not use Brightdesk branding in a way that is misleading, impersonates the project, or suggests sponsorship or approval that has not been given.
- Factual references needed to identify the source of MIT-licensed code remain subject to the applicable licence and law.

## Original brand assets

Use original Brightdesk artwork only in accordance with the project's published asset and release rules. Third-party fonts, icons, images and other assets must retain their own required attribution and licence notices. A Brightdesk label does not convert third-party material into Brightdesk-owned material.

## No registration claim

This policy records the project's intended identity and release-presentation rules. It does not claim that the Brightdesk name or any logo is registered as a trademark, and it is not a legal opinion about ownership or enforceability in any country.

## Authenticity

Checksums, signed packages and provenance records can help users identify official releases. They do not make local copies impossible to modify. Release-signing and customer-side verification must not be described as active until they have actually been implemented and tested.
